Euler's identity is often called the most beautiful equation in mathematics:

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$

It links five fundamental constants — $e$, $i$, $\pi$, $1$, and $0$ — in a single
statement. It falls out of Euler's formula,

$$
e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta
$$

evaluated at $\theta = \pi$, where $\cos\pi = -1$ and $\sin\pi = 0$.
